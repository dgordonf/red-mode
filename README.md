<h1 align="center">Red Mode</h1>

<p align="center">One click and your whole Windows screen goes red. Click again and it's back to normal.</p>

---

Looking at a bright screen at night stinks. Red mode makes this better.

Pilots, astronomers and the military use red light to keep their vison at night. Now you can too.

## Install

Windows 10 or 11. No admin needed.

1. Download or clone this repo.
2. Right-click `install.ps1` and choose **Run with PowerShell**.

You'll get a **Red Mode** icon on your Desktop.

## Use

Double-click the icon or press **Ctrl + Alt + R**. Do it again to turn it off.

Turn your brightness down too. Dim red is the goal.

## Heads up

- DRM video and HDR screens may not turn red.
- If you kill it from Task Manager your pointer stays red. Toggle it on and off again to fix it.

## Uninstall

Run `uninstall.ps1`.
